    Population Specialty Group

    The AAG’s Population Specialty Group promotes research, teaching, and service in the general field of population geography. Its purpose is to stimulate the exchange of information among members of PSG, encourage the development of population geography as a science and a profession, and develop close relations and interchange with other sciences, especially those dealing with population.

    About the Population Specialty Group

    The Population Specialty Group (PSG) is a specialty group of the American Association of Geographers (AAG). Members study population distribution, migration, fertility, mortality, health, urbanization, and related topics from a geographic perspective.

    2027 AAG PSG Student Paper Competition

    Extended deadline: December 15, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time

    The Population Specialty Group (PSG) is currently accepting applications for the 2027 AAG PSG Student Paper Competition. The competition is open to any student (graduate or undergraduate) who is a current PSG member and will be attending the 2027 AAG Annual Meeting in New York City.

    To be considered, students should submit a paper (between 2,500 and 8,000 words) on any topic of population geography through the Google form below. The paper must be based on original work.

    The winning paper will receive an award of $300. Honorable mention prizes of $100 may also be awarded.
